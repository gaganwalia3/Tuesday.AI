# 🌐 Web Research

## Why?

AI models don't always have the latest information.

Our assistant needs to research current information.

We use Tavily for web search.

---

## Tool

searchWeb()

Gemini can decide when it needs current information.

Example:

User:

"What are the latest developments in AI?"

Gemini:

"I need current information."

↓

searchWeb()

↓

Tavily

↓

Search results

↓

Gemini analyzes results

↓

Final answer
